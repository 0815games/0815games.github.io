# Patch 4: progression pacing review

This is an arithmetic review of the existing economy, **not a playtest or balance change**. Completed waves only; losses partway through a wave provide no XP or permanent loot. XP persists across runs. Co-op/1v1 participants use the same per-wave XP formula when their wave receipt is credited, but participation can differ.

| Waves completed in one fresh run | Total XP | Character level | Permanent equipment unlocked on first clear |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 30 | 1 | none |
| 2 | 70 | 1 | Field Scope |
| 5 | 250 | 1 | Scope, Vest |
| 6 | 330 | 2 | + Boots |
| 10 | 750 | 3 | + Binoculars, Radio |
| 20 | 2,500 | 5 | all 10 items |
| 50 | 13,750 | 13 | no further equipment |
| 100 | 52,500 | 26 | no further equipment |

- A fresh soldier gets their first skill point after 300 XP, equivalent to wave 6 in one uninterrupted run, or earlier across repeated runs. Completing wave 5 alone leaves 50 XP to level 2, so the first boss can be finished without an active ability. This deserves new-player testing; don't change the level-2 skill rule without evidence.
- Base scrap is 10 per completed wave, plus 10 when the wave's item is already owned. A basic rank-1 bunker upgrade costs 30; the advanced pair costs 50. Repeating low waves provides steady income, but short runs cannot receive items from higher wave milestones. Loot is deterministic, not random.
- Equipment milestones stop at wave 20. XP per wave rises by 10 until wave 100, then remains at 1,020, while each level's XP requirement continues increasing by 150. Late-game points therefore take progressively more completed waves. The skill tree is much larger than the early levels can fill; actual session length and completion rates need playtesting before retuning rewards.
- These figures assume every indicated wave is credited and no XP cap or disconnected participant intervenes. The report separates the wave reward value from confirmed transactions. Server-authoritative progression and real-device multiplayer testing remain out of scope for this patch.
