How to play

Tap an empty pad to build, or a tower to upgrade it. Towers have six levels; choose a permanent specialization at level 3. Upgrade costs increase at each level. Tap open ground to move your hero; on desktop use WASD or arrow keys. The hero fires automatically. Airstrike damages all enemies and recharges in 30 seconds of game time. Start each wave manually. Scouts (yellow triangles) are fast, tanks (brown squares) are armored, and aircraft (blue arrows) fly along the same route. Infantry are orange circles. MGs and snipers excel against infantry; cannons counter tanks; Flak counters aircraft. All towers can hit all enemies, but with different damage.
