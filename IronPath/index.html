1 vs 1 uses two equally sized, separate battlefields with the same waves.

Complete waves together to earn XP. While both players are active, enemies spawn with 50% more health and each player receives 65% of base supply rewards (rounded down). If your teammate leaves, newly spawned enemies and rewards return to solo rates until they rejoin.

MULTIPLAYER PARTYONLINE

Share one code. The host approves up to three players and chooses a mode. Two players can play co-op or 1 vs 1; three can play large-map co-op; four can play co-op or 2 vs 2. Versus is a survival race, not direct combat.

Create a party, open Friends, then invite an accepted friend. They can join directly; players using a party code still need approval.

Solo combat pauses while the lobby is open. Online matches continue independently.

Connection & safety tips
  • Everyone should reload the game after an update and use the same current version.
  • Four-digit codes can be guessed. Share them privately and approve only players you know.
  • Keep the host’s game open. Ordinary parties do not survive a host reload; host recovery is an experimental opt-in.
  • Competitive matches pause while someone is disconnected. Results and rewards are not server-verified or ranked.

      How to play

      Tap a BUILD marker to choose a tower from the build panel. Tap a tower to see its range and open its upgrade and sell panel. Unaffordable choices are greyed out; tap them to read their details. Confirm builds in the details window and confirm upgrades in the tower panel. Selling returns half the total investment. Tap open ground to deselect and move your hero. Towers have six levels; choose a permanent specialization at level 3. Upgrade costs increase at each level. Tap open ground to move your hero; on desktop use WASD or arrow keys. The hero fires automatically. Choose a skill path ability in SKILLS; abilities normally recharge in 30 seconds of game time. Start each wave manually. Scouts are fast, tanks are armored, and aircraft fly along the same route as infantry. MGs and snipers excel against infantry; cannons counter tanks; Flak counters aircraft. All towers can hit all enemies, but with different damage.
