COMMAND CENTER

Battle controls

How to play

Tap a plus pad to choose a tower from the build panel. Tap a tower to see its range and open its upgrade and sell panel. Unaffordable choices are greyed out; tap them to read their details. Confirm purchases in the details window. Selling returns half the total investment. Tap open ground to deselect and move your hero. Towers have six levels; choose a permanent specialization at level 3. Upgrade costs increase at each level. Tap open ground to move your hero; on desktop use WASD or arrow keys. The hero fires automatically. Choose a skill path ability in SKILLS; abilities recharge in 30 seconds of game time. Start each wave manually. Scouts (yellow triangles) are fast, tanks (brown squares) are armored, and aircraft (blue arrows) fly along the same route. Infantry are orange circles. MGs and snipers excel against infantry; cannons counter tanks; Flak counters aircraft. All towers can hit all enemies, but with different damage.
