# Community leaderboard rollout

The board shows the top 50 signed-in players by highest **completed** wave in solo Endless or co-op, separately, and can filter to accepted friends. A personal best is visible even outside the top 50. Campaign and versus results do not count. Each account contributes its own entry; duplicate names can be distinguished by a partial player ID. Scores begin accumulating after this client is deployed and are not backfilled from prior runs. Solo submissions happen on wave completion; co-op submissions use the local player's host-reported completed-wave masks. Best scores retry while the game remains open; an offline score can be lost if the page closes before a save succeeds.

All scores are **client-reported and unverified**; a player can submit any allowed value, and player-hosted co-op is also untrusted. Never use this board for prizes, anti-cheat, or trustworthy competitive rewards. Authenticated players can read score names, IDs, and waves, but not private profiles. Rules enforce account ownership, known mode, profile name, integer bounds, and nondecreasing scores—not score legitimacy. No server-side rate limiting, blocking, or abuse moderation is provided.

`leaderboards.complete.draft.rules.json` is the entire replacement based on `coop-invites.complete.draft.rules.json`, which the user reported publishing. Back up and compare the current live rules and merge independent changes; publish only the fully reviewed replacement after emulator tests pass and **before** deploying the client. Publication replaces all RTDB rules. We cannot independently inspect or publish the live rules from this environment.

After deployment, test two real signed-in accounts: complete solo waves, reload and check personal best and ordering, accept a friendship and verify the friends filter, play co-op and verify the co-op board updates separately; verify story and versus do not update either board, retry after a disconnect, and test mobile. Automated/emulator tests are not a substitute for live device testing.
