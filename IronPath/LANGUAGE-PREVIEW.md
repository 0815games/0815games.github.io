# Language switch: work in progress

This branch contains a **preview**, not a complete German localization. The selector persists only a local browser preference and does not alter account, progression, multiplayer data, player names or codes. Marked static text, the command center, major battlefield controls and field-upgrade descriptions, much of the multiplayer lobby, the interactive tutorial, the armory equipment, bunker upgrade and service-medal cards, equipment names in the command center and friend dossier, parts of friends, notifications, leaderboards and multiplayer speed controls can switch between English and German; the interface explicitly warns German users that text remains untranslated.

Continue translating and testing every player-facing surface in incremental releases: intro, authentication and errors; remaining campaign controls and story status messages; the battle HUD, towers, abilities, perks, terrain, results and notifications; skills and armory data; tutorial and practice; Friends, invitations, scores and all multiplayer setup/status/recovery flows; accessibility labels, placeholders and confirmations. Preserve state and in-progress drafts when the language changes. Test both languages, narrow layouts, text overflow, reload and storage failures, and actual two-device multiplayer. Never translate names, identifiers, saved values, or numeric game rules. Translation needs human review; retain the visible preview warning until coverage is complete.

The armory cards and confirmations now switch between English and German while preserving the selected tab and saved data. Some armory status messages already on screen, battle and other surfaces may still remain in English. Battle results, parts of the tower menu and selected battlefield notices now switch languages. Translation needs human review.

The five core skill paths and their main and specialist ability descriptions are translated. Advanced mastery, hybrid and doctrine names, effects and prerequisites are translated. The build summary and skill draft confirmations are translated; other skill text may remain in English. Campaign titles, briefings, objectives and cinematic dialogue are translated, though some controls and messages may still be English. The flag selector identifies the German option as a preview; the preview notice remains available to assistive technology.
